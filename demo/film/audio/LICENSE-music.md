## Ruled out
- **FreePD.com:** the site is offline (checked 2026-11-08).
- **FMA tracks under CC BY-NC or BY-ND:** a launch promo is promotional use, so NC is unsafe, and ND blocks
  editing. This rules out Scott Holmes, AudioCoffee, Lite Saturation, Gregor Quendel and Denys Kyshchuk.
- **Pixabay:** the license would work (no attribution needed). However, the search results pages give no
  direct file links to fetch, and some Pixabay tracks are registered with YouTube Content ID. If you pick
  one by hand, check that its page doesn't mention Content ID.
